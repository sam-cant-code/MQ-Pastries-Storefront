Accessibility Statement

MQ Pastries is committed to making our website usable and accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. We are continually working to improve the experience for all visitors and to apply relevant accessibility standards where possible.

If you encounter any difficulty using this site — whether navigating pages, reading content, or completing an order — please let us know. We take this feedback seriously and will do our best to address it.
